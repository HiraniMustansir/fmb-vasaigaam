# Committee operator guide

1. **Register or sign in.** New members choose Create account, enter email/password, then their name and committee position and tap Finish setup. No email code, invitation or approval is required. The first setup creates the workspace; subsequent members automatically join it. Existing members sign in with their password. If an old account used email codes, ask the project administrator to set its password.
2. **Set up.** In Home → Members, the owner configures kitchen defaults. Add suppliers in Stock → Suppliers, ingredients in Stock, and recipes in Menu → Recipes. Enter opening stock with its cost, then opening cash/bank using Accounts → Record money → Opening. Home shows setup records still missing.
3. **Plan food.** Add a menu or Miqaat. Tiffin counts mean tiffins; gathering counts mean thaals. For 23 thaals at 8 people each, plan 184 people before buffer. Set the preparation buffer once. Add one or more recipe dishes. Observance-only records create no demand. Copy a service to start a new plan, then change its date before posting.
4. **Shop.** Stock → Shopping combines current food demand across services. It compares demand with physical stock. Supplier bills are separate from receipt and payment. Review existing bills before placing new orders; this version does not track purchase orders or confirmed incoming supply.
5. **Receive.** Record a supplier bill with lines in ingredient base units. Stock → Record movement → Receipt requires its invoice line and the quantity actually received. Enter batch/expiry if known. Partial receipts are allowed; over-receipts are blocked. Supplier returns use a negative quantity and the original invoice line.
6. **Cook.** Menu → Cooking lets you reserve ingredients and record actual consumption. Publishing a plan does not deduct stock. Enter the actual ingredients and prepared, distributed, leftover, redistributed and wasted counts. Counts use the service's unit (tiffins or thaals), including leftover/waste counts. Keep Complete false for partial cooking, then post the remaining output later. For an additional run after completion, choose Amendment true and explain why.
7. **Pay.** Accounts → Pay supplier records a partial or full invoice payment. Do not enter the payment again as a general expense. The outstanding invoice amount updates separately. General operating expenses, contributions, member advances and reimbursements have their own types. Member Expense records an amount owed to a member; Reimbursement pays that balance. Settlement records spend against an advance already issued.
8. **Correct.** Stock count posts the difference between actual counted stock and the ledger, with a reason. Reverse movement restores the original quantity and linked value history, subject to available stock. Accounts → Journal → Reverse record corrects supported money postings. Original records remain. Invoice cancellation and consumption reversal are not yet supported; consult the project owner before recording an incorrect bill or cooking run.
9. **Coordinate.** Home → Tasks records assignments and due dates. Menu → Notes records notes. Home → Activity shows shared changes. Owner-maintained calendar dates include source and verification; unmapped dates are unverified. Local gatherings remain independent from observances.
10. **Work offline.** Entry forms save local drafts after a short pause and on Close draft. Drafts are not posted. Home → Drafts lets you review and retry after reconnecting. On an edit conflict, refresh and deliberately re-enter/review against current records. Do not change an uncertain posting's key or create a replacement until checking whether the original succeeded.
11. **Evidence and export.** Accounts → Receipts uploads JPEG/PNG/PDF evidence up to 10 MB. Supply the bill number or expense reference. Opening evidence creates a short-lived private link. It does not post stock or money. Menu Share and Journal Share CSV invoke Android's share sheet only when tapped.
12. **Access.** The owner can revoke access and transfer ownership. New members register themselves. All active positions can edit stock, menus and finances. Sign out on shared devices. A revoked member loses server access immediately; an offline device clears its cache when membership is next checked.
